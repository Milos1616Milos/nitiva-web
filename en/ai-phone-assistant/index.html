An AI assistant that picks up the phone when you can't

At work or in a meeting you can't answer every call. The AI assistant takes the call, finds out what the customer needs and when it suits them to be contacted, and sends you a short summary on Telegram. You then call back yourself.

Arrange a no-obligation consultation

Who it is for

  • Sole traders and small businesses whose customers call while they are busy working.
  • Those who don't want to lose enquiries because of an unanswered phone.
  • Businesses that want to be clear about what the assistant may and may not say.

How it works

  • The customer calls a separate number that I set up for you.
  • The assistant introduces itself as an AI, says the call is being recorded and asks how it can help.
  • It finds out what the customer needs and when it suits them to be called back.
  • You receive a short summary on Telegram, not a full transcript of the call.
  • It promises nothing: it only says that you will get in touch.

Price

As an add-on to a website or social media from CZK 490 per month, plus a one-off setup of the number and the assistant. I set the exact price according to the number of calls after a short consultation. I am not a VAT payer.

The rules I stand by

  • The assistant always introduces itself as an AI and mentions that the call is recorded.
  • It does not collect payment details, personal ID numbers or health data.
  • No AI system is perfect, so I check the results at the start and tune the assistant.
  • Every customer has their own number, their own assistant and their own delivery of summaries.

Frequently asked questions

Does the customer know they are talking to an AI?

Yes. At the start of the call the assistant introduces itself as an AI and says that the call is recorded. I don't deploy it without that.

What do I receive after a call?

A short summary: what the customer needs and when it suits them for you to get in touch. Not a full transcript of the call.

Does the assistant promise prices or appointments?

No. I set it up to promise nothing and to say only that you will get in touch. Prices and appointments are for you to agree.

What should callers not say?

The assistant is not meant to collect card numbers, personal ID numbers or health data. We agree in advance with you what it may ask about.

Can I try it out?

Yes. Get in touch and I will show you how the assistant handles a call.

Privacy policy

Valid from 4 October 2026, updated 29 October 2026. Short and clear: what we store about you, why, and how you can have it deleted.

1. Who processes the data

The controller of personal data is Miloš Brisuda, doing business under the Nitiva brand, Business ID (IČO) 29824125, Kubelíkova 1258/43, 130 00 Praha 3, Czech Republic. Contact: milos@nitiva.cz.

2. What data and why

The contact form on the website. The name, e-mail and message text that you fill in yourself. We use them only to answer your enquiry and, where relevant, to offer our service.

Chat on the website. The text of your messages in the chat. The chat answers automatically using an artificial intelligence program, and its answers are not binding. If you leave us your contact details in the chat or via the form, a person will get in touch with you.

Messages on Facebook and Instagram (Messenger, direct messages). The text of your message, your public name and the account identifier provided to us by Meta. We use them solely to reply to your message. The reply may be prepared by an automated system; for now, a person approves it before it is sent.

The legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract and our legitimate interest in answering customers' enquiries. We do not use the data for automated decision-making with legal effects and we do not sell it.

3. Who we pass it to

The data is processed by providers we need to operate: Meta (Facebook and Instagram, the platforms through which messages arrive), the hosting provider of our server, the Telegram service, which sends us internal notifications about new enquiries, and the provider of the language model that helps to draft a reply. Some of these services may process data outside the EU, on the basis of standard contractual clauses or another lawful mechanism.

4. How long we keep the data

We keep enquiries from the form and from messages for at most 12 months from the last contact, or until you ask us to delete them. If you become a customer, we keep the data needed for the contract and for accounting for the period required by law.

5. Your rights

You have the right of access to your data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, portability and the right to object. Just write to milos@nitiva.cz. You can lodge a complaint with the Office for Personal Data Protection of the Czech Republic (uoou.cz).

6. How to ask for your data to be deleted

Write to milos@nitiva.cz from the e-mail address, or with a link to the Facebook or Instagram account, from which you wrote to us, and say that you want your data deleted. We will delete the data within 30 days and confirm it to you by e-mail. You can also delete messages on Facebook and Instagram yourself; we will delete our copy at your request.

7. Cookies

Until you decide, we do not turn marketing cookies on. Your browser remembers the choice “accept” or “reject” until you change it with the Cookie settings link in the footer, or until you delete this website’s data. That choice is not tracking.

We measure only anonymous visit statistics: the number of visits, the page visited and the source you came from. This happens without cookies and without storing anything on your device. A one-way code is created from your IP address and browser; it changes every day and cannot be used to find out who you are. We do not store the IP address itself. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in knowing whether the website works and where people come to us from.

8. Meta Pixel

If you choose Accept in the cookie bar, we load the Meta Pixel, identifier 164438667301853. The purpose is to measure how well our ads work: a page view and, after a form is sent successfully, a lead event. We do not send your name, e-mail or message text to Meta. The browser does pass Meta technical connection data, such as the page address and IP address.

The controller is Miloš Brisuda, doing business under the Nitiva brand, as stated in section 1. The legal basis is your consent. The recipient is Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd. Meta may transfer data to the USA under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

You can withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it: open Cookie settings in the footer and choose Reject. That stops further measurement. The cookie Meta stores after consent usually lasts at most 90 days. The consent choice itself stays in your browser until you change or delete it. We delete other data as described in section 6 when you write to milos@nitiva.cz.

A website for your small business that makes it easy for customers to get in touch

A good website doesn't have to be big. It has to say quickly what you do, for whom, and how to reach you. I build a clear website that works on mobile and has a foundation for Google search.

Arrange a no-obligation consultation

Who it is for

  • Sole traders and small businesses that have no website, or whose current one brings no enquiries.
  • Businesses that want clear copy about the customer, not about themselves.
  • Those who need a website quickly and without unnecessary complexity.

What I take care of on the website

  • A clear message at the top of the page: what you do and for whom.
  • A call to get in touch in the right places.
  • Fast loading and a comfortable display on mobile.
  • Basic SEO: titles, descriptions, headings, sitemap and structured data.
  • Connection to social media and, if needed, to an AI assistant.

How the collaboration works

  • Consultation: I find out what the website should do and what is missing today.
  • Design: the page structure and the main copy.
  • Delivery and launch: I set up the website, test it and put it live.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost?

I don't list a price in advance because it depends on scope and needs. After a short consultation I send you a specific offer.

Can you handle a website I already have?

Yes. I can adjust the copy, structure or technical setup of an existing website if it makes sense.

Will you help people find me on Google?

I prepare the technical and content foundation for search. Google rankings can't be guaranteed in advance, and results show up over weeks to months.

Will I be able to edit the website myself?

We agree it based on what you need. Often it is enough that I make the changes for you after you send them by e-mail.

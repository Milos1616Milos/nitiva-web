Frequently asked questions

Before you decide

Do I need to understand technology?

No. Send us the price list, photos and a few details. We handle the rest and explain it in plain language.

What if the responder answers wrongly?

It answers only from the information you give us. When it isn't sure, it doesn't make things up but passes the message on to you. We agree the wording of the answers together before launch.

Is this compliant with personal data protection rules?

We store only what the customer writes themselves and use it to handle their enquiry. We add the specific terms to the contract before launch.

How do I end the collaboration?

Send us an e-mail. There is no notice period and no penalty.

Scope of the collaboration

What the monthly service includes

Content and publishing

Every month we prepare 8 posts with copy and static graphics or your photograph. We adapt the content for one Facebook page and one Instagram account; these are 8 content drafts, not 16 different posts. One shared round of comments is included. We correct our own mistakes at no extra charge. We schedule and publish approved content, provided we have the necessary access to your accounts. We verify the possibility of publishing before you order. Photography, videos, Reels and paid advertising are not part of this scope.

Hosting and your domain

Website hosting is included in the monthly price. The domain is yours, and you pay for its registration or renewal separately at the registrar's price, announced in advance. If you already have a domain, we will use it. Setting up one domain is included in the setup fee.

Minor updates

The monthly service includes one set of minor updates to existing information on the website and in the responder, up to 20 minutes of work. We quote any larger change first and start only after your approval.

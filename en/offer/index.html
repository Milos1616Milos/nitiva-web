Before you decide

Do I need to understand technology?

No. Send me the price list, photos and a few details. I handle the rest and explain it in plain language.

What if the responder gets an answer wrong?

It answers only from the information you give me. When it isn't sure, it doesn't make things up but passes the message on to you. We agree the wording of the answers together before launch.

Is this compliant with personal data protection rules?

I store only what the customer writes themselves and use it to handle their enquiry. I add the specific terms to the contract before launch.

How do I end the collaboration?

Send me an e-mail. There is no notice period and no penalty.

What the monthly service includes

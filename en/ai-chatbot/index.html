An AI chatbot for your website that answers your customers

Customers ask on Sunday evening and in the middle of the working day. An AI assistant answers everyday questions from verified information, collects the contact and hands anything complicated over to you.

Arrange a no-obligation consultation

Who it is for

  • Small businesses and e-shops whose customers keep asking the same questions.
  • Those who can't reply immediately and don't want to lose enquiries.
  • Businesses that want to be clear about what the chatbot may and may not say.

What the chatbot does

  • Answers common questions based on information you approve.
  • Collects the enquirer's contact details and e-mails them to you.
  • When it doesn't know, it says so and hands the question to a person.
  • Works in Czech and Slovak.
  • You can try a demo on the Czech version of this website, in the bottom-right corner.

How the deployment works

  • Consultation: I write down your customers' most frequent questions.
  • Setup: I prepare the answers and the rules for what the chatbot must not promise.
  • Launch and tuning: I embed the chatbot into your website and adjust it based on real enquiries.

Frequently asked questions

Does the chatbot make up answers?

I set it up to answer only from information you approve and to pass the question to you when it doesn't know the answer. No AI system is perfect, so I check the results.

Does the chatbot also work on Instagram or Facebook?

Connecting to social networks is technically possible; it depends on your account and settings. I will tell you honestly what can and can't be done.

What happens to customers' data?

The chatbot collects only what the customer writes. I use the data to answer the enquiry. We agree the details in advance.

Can I try the chatbot?

Yes. The Czech version of nitiva.cz has a demo assistant that answers questions about my services.

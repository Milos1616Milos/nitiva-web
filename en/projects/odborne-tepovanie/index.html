Odborné tepovanie: a website that works for the owner

A family business from Bánovce nad Bebravou cleans sofas, carpets and mattresses at customers' homes and in companies. Its original website was just a simple landing page. We built a new one that shows the price straight away, takes an order and answers questions.

Custom websitePrice calculatorAI assistantForms → e-mail

The website is ready to be deployed on the company's own domain

From a single page to a complete website

The customer knows the price before they call

  • A price calculator. The customer enters what they want cleaned and immediately sees an indicative price, including transport.
  • A price list based on reality. We took the prices from the company's own price list, with no invented items.
  • Online ordering. A short form; the enquiry goes straight to the owner's e-mail.
  • Large jobs. A separate section and form for offices, schools, kindergartens and vehicle fleets.
  • A smart assistant. It answers only from verified information, doesn't make things up and hands more complex enquiries to a person.
  • Fast and mobile-friendly. A single page without heavy libraries, with the gallery and calculator adapted for phones.

Even huge jobs have their place

The company often cleans large areas of office carpet, dozens of chairs or school premises. The new website says so clearly and offers a separate form.

What we know so far and what we will add

We don't give specific figures (number of enquiries, traffic) here yet, because the new website hasn't been running for long. We will add them once they are verified. Before-and-after photos of cleaning and customer reviews will be added gradually, and the website is ready for them.

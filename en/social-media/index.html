Social media management for small businesses and sole traders

Facebook and Instagram don't have to eat your evenings. I prepare the plan, write the posts, create consistent graphics and take care of regularity. You get on with your craft.

Arrange a no-obligation consultation

Who it is for

  • Sole traders and small businesses that have a Facebook or Instagram profile but can't keep up with posting.
  • Businesses that want their profile to look trustworthy when a customer looks them up.
  • Those who want enquiries from their posts, not just "likes".

What you get

  • A content plan based on what your customers really care about.
  • Copy in Czech or Slovak without unnecessary jargon.
  • Consistent graphics in your colours, so people recognise you.
  • Regular publishing on Facebook and Instagram.
  • A clear call to get in touch in your posts and in your profile.

How the collaboration works

  • Short consultation: I ask what you do, for whom and what takes up your time today.
  • Proposed approach: I tell you honestly what makes sense and what doesn't.
  • Delivery: I prepare the posts, you approve them, and I publish them.

Frequently asked questions

How often will posts be published?

I set the frequency according to your capacity and your trade. Regularity matters more than quantity. It's better to post less, but consistently.

Do I have to supply materials?

Photos of your work are enough, if you have them, plus a few sentences about what you do. We handle the rest together in a short consultation.

Can I approve posts before they are published?

Yes. I show you the posts in advance and publish them only after your approval.

Do you promise a specific number of followers or enquiries?

No. Follower growth and the number of enquiries can't be guaranteed in advance. I guarantee regularity, quality and a clear process.
